How I think

My design
philosophy

🔍

Curiosity first

Every great design starts with a dumb question. I resist jumping to solutions before truly understanding the problem — even when the deadline screams otherwise.

🪄

Simple ≠ easy

The simplest interface is the hardest to design. I chase clarity obsessively — removing friction, trimming noise, making the complex feel effortless.

💬

Empathy is the method

Numbers tell me what. People tell me why. Real research, real users, real conversations — that's where the real insights hide.
